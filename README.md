# Lore — The DM's Perspective

One place for everything about your world: places, people, powers, things and history. Write entries, link them to
each other, keep secrets only you can see, and reveal entries to your players when they discover them.

Part of The DM's Perspective app suite: https://thedmsperspective.github.io

- Your world is kept on your device and privately in your own Google Drive.
- Pictures go in a "Lore pictures" folder in your Drive, viewable by link.
- Players at your table see only what you've revealed, live.

No world comes with the app: you build your own.
